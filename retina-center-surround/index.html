Simplified, illustrative model with one center cone and six surround cones. Numbers are approximate. Light is shown entering from the top for clarity; in the eye it passes through the ganglion and bipolar layers before reaching the photoreceptors. Horizontal cell feedback onto cones is shown as inhibitory, classically attributed to GABA; protons and ephaptic coupling have also been proposed. Circled signs mark whether each synapse preserves (+) or inverts (−) the presynaptic voltage change. Amacrine cells, omitted here, add further surround inhibition in the inner retina.
