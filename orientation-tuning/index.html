Primary visual cortex (V1)

Click a neuron to record from it. Lighter dots in layer 4 are simple cells; darker dots in the other layers are complex cells.

Simplified, illustrative model. Every neuron here shares the same receptive-field location, as neighboring columns do. Simple cells sum 4 ON-center LGN inputs whose centers line up; complex cells sum the rectified outputs of 7 simple cells with the same preference at neighboring positions. Placing simple cells in layer 4 and complex cells in the other layers is a common simplification; both types occur in several layers. Orientation is measured counterclockwise from horizontal, so 90° is vertical.
