Simplified, illustrative rate model of the circuit on the slides. Medium spiny neurons are silent unless driven by cortex; dopamine raises the gain of D1 MSNs and lowers the gain of D2 MSNs. Brief random bursts of cortical activity stand in for competing motor programs, which a healthy circuit keeps suppressed. Modeling Tourette’s as excess dopamine signaling is a simplification of one leading hypothesis. Deep brain stimulation is modeled as blocking the subthalamic nucleus’s pathological output. Resting tremor and other symptoms are not modeled.
