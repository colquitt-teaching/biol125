Cross-section at T10

Simplified, illustrative model. Spinal segments are shown, not vertebral levels (the cord is shorter than the spine, so segments sit higher than the vertebrae of the same name). Pain fibers are modeled as rising one segment before crossing, which is why pain loss on the opposite side begins just below a lesion. Dermatome boundaries are approximate and neighboring dermatomes overlap in real patients. Only sensation is modeled: lesions that cut the lateral corticospinal tract also cause weakness or paralysis below the lesion.
