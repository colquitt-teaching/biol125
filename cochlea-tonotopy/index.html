Basilar membrane stiffness

Normally the membrane is narrow and stiff at the base and wide and floppy at the apex. Each place resonates best at a frequency set by its stiffness.

×1 100%

Simplified, illustrative model of a 35 mm human cochlea. The place–frequency map follows the Greenwood function; stiffness changes shift it on the assumption that each place resonates at a frequency proportional to the square root of its stiffness. The active amplification by outer hair cells is not modeled separately. Hair cell AC responses are filtered by the membrane’s time constant, so above about 1 kHz the DC component dominates. Auditory nerve fibers can phase-lock up to a few kHz.
