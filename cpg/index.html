Simplified, illustrative rate model based on the slide’s circuit. Each E/R population bursts on its own because of strong recurrent excitation and slow adaptation; the two halves differ slightly in their natural speed, and reciprocal inhibition locks them in alternation. Motor neurons also receive a weak steady excitation, which the rIa interneurons normally suppress during the antagonist’s phase. Rhythms here are slowed so they are easy to follow.
