BIOL125

Introduction to Neuroscience: interactive simulations

Explore the circuits from lecture by changing the inputs yourself and watching how each cell responds. Each simulation runs in your browser and works on laptops, tablets and phones.

  • Lecture 9: Somatosensation

    Spinal cord lesions and sensory loss

    Place hemisections, complete transections, dorsal root ganglion lesions and more, then test pain and vibration on the body to see why touch and pain are lost on different sides.

  • Lecture 10: Vision

    Building a center–surround receptive field

    Shine light on center and surround cones and follow the signal through horizontal, bipolar and ganglion cells to see how lateral inhibition makes retinal ganglion cells detect contrast.

  • Lecture 11: Vision 2

    Orientation tuning in V1: simple and complex cells

    Recreate Hubel and Wiesel’s experiment: rotate and move a bar of light, build tuning curves for simple and complex cells, and drive electrodes through the cortex to discover orientation columns.

New simulations are added as the course goes on.
